## Keyword gaps vs. site structure

**Gap 1: No "best [product]" pages.** The site has starter kits but no dedicated product category pages. Wirecutter wins these. We need at least 3–5 individual review pages to compete.

**Gap 2: No comparison pages.** "X vs Y" is high-intent traffic that converts well. Zero coverage currently.

**Gap 3: Pickleball is underserved overall.** The sport is growing fastest. We have one starter kit page and one blog post. Opportunity to become an authority before big publishers move in.

**Gap 4: Gift keywords.** "fitness gifts for [sport]" are addressable with our existing /gifts page but need SEO work (title, H1, internal links from starter kit pages).
